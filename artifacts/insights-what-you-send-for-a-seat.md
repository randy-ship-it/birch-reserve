Most ad networks ask for a banner and a bid.

That is the wrong first file for a room a brand already paid to fill. The customer already checked out, booked, or opened a plan. The next unit has to look like it belongs on that shelf, not like it escaped a feed.

Birch Reserve sells the aisle, not the auction. Eight category seats sit inside signed Scale Health hubs. You pick one aisle. You do not buy a stack of websites.

Here is the actual handoff after you look or lock.

Send four things.

1. A square still of the product as it should sit after checkout or on a plan. No lifestyle collage. No tiny legal text on the image.
2. One destination URL. The page the member should open next. Not a homepage dump.
3. The aisle: pain relief, recovery hardware, nutrition, sleep, meal prep, women's health, men's health, or diagnostics.
4. A contact who can approve an insertion order in 72 hours.

Optional: a silent 15-second motion cut of the same product. No voiceover. No claim about results.

Nothing runs when the card clears. The payment is a media credit. The insertion order has to name the hub and the surface: post-checkout, plan, booking, or member hub. If the still does not fit the room, we say so before anyone designs around it.
