1Randy's feedback

From the team thread on the first cut of the brand partners page (scalehealth.ca/brand-partners). One note so far; the checklist below turns it into things to verify.

What Randy said

Make the part about where your products go match our intake. It is not positioned for gyms and clinics. It is three channels:

  1. Branded ScaleHealth.ca hubs
  2. Our private retail network of fitness and recovery retailers
  3. Our owned digital clinic, Calm Joints, and our Street Team Ambassadors

Stat chips and the fine print for each channel live on our intake (Where your products go). Copy them verbatim from there; do not restate, round or extend them anywhere else.

Checklist

  • "Where your products go" lists exactly those three channels, in that order, with the same names as our intake.
  • Nothing on the page positions the offer for gyms or clinics as the audience for products.
  • The channel stat chips and the "modelled estimates from listed partners, reach and audience, not delivered impressions" fine print are copied verbatim from the live intake.
  • Look and feel follows the tokens in section 2, in the Scale variant on scalehealth.ca.
  • Any form that collects brand details uses the field names, labels, helper text and validation in section 5.
  • The brand-partner submit hands data over as described in section 6.
  • Section 7 do / don't list is clean on every page.

Status when this page was written: the live brand partners page already shows the three channels in this order. Re-check the stat chips and the fine print against the intake.

6Data handoff

What exists today, and what to build if the brand flow needs to pass data to us.

What exists

  • The intake posts to our own same-origin routes on birchreserve.net (and through the mirror on scalehealth.ca/intake): POST /v1/brand-intake (create or first autosave), PATCH /v1/brand-intake/:id (merge changes, or submit), POST /v1/brand-intake/:id/kit, GET /v1/brand-categories.
  • Body is JSON, up to 64 KB. Nothing is sent without a valid email on a submit. Partial saves need a valid email. Responses are never cached.
  • Auth: no login for visitors. Each record gets a per-record edit token returned on create and sent back in the X-Edit-Token header on later updates. The routes forward to the CRM server-side with a shared-secret header that never reaches the browser.
  • Supplier hub requests are stored as "pending approval". Only Randy or Jon approves; nothing publishes from the browser.

What does not exist

  • No partner webhook, no partner API key, no documented external contract. The routes above are the site's own form plumbing and their body shape is strict and can change. Do not post to them from another app.
  • No endpoint that sends a brand's data to a third-party form.

Simplest working handoff today: send brands to https://www.scalehealth.ca/intake/supplier?brand=Your%20Brand. They get the same look and flow, and the request lands with us.

Suggested payload if a server-to-server handoff is wanted

A proposal only, for Randy and Jon to approve before anything is built. Authentication would be a signed request (HMAC signature header plus timestamp) with a secret kept in the host's environment variables, never in code or the browser.

7Do / don't

Do

  • Describe Scale as a distribution platform, starting in Recovery.
  • Use "Scale Health" on Scale pages and "Birch Reserve" on Birch pages. Chat name on Scale is "Scale Squirrel".
  • Keep claims plain and true. Label estimates as estimates, state USD when money appears.
  • Say "reach and audience, not delivered impressions" wherever the channel figures appear.
  • Copy figures only from the live intake, verbatim.
  • Say "free monetization kit" naturally where the kit is offered.
  • Say a person reviews every request and nothing is booked or charged automatically.
  • Keep the page quick: website and work email first, everything else optional.

Don't

  • Don't add counts, audience sizes, outcomes or forecasts that are not already on our intake.
  • Don't promise results, placement, rankings or revenue.
  • Don't name other companies, ventures or partners that are not on the public site, and don't mention internal tools, systems or vendors.
  • Don't describe Scale as anything other than a distribution platform starting in Recovery.
  • Don't position the offer for gyms and clinics as the buyers of products. Hubs are where products appear.
  • Don't show or hint at any promo code.
  • Don't put secrets, keys, tokens or store credentials anywhere public.
  • Don't edit the production copy of the intake from this page. Ask first.
