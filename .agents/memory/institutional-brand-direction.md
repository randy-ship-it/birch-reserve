---
name: Institutional brand direction
description: The durable visual and conversion standard for Birch Reserve's major-brand audience.
---

Birch Reserve should feel institutional, procurement-ready, and credible while still carrying a distinctive editorial sales voice. Position it concretely as a private digital and physical media and activation network across closed customer environments and participating health and wellness locations. Use Fraunces with IBM Plex Sans, navy #071A39, and lime #C8F55A. Comparisons should read as bold editorial contrasts, not tables.

Use real, approved Scale Health hub captures as proof. Never generate AI stand-ins for hubs or imply that a signed/launching hub is already live. Describe digital inventory as customer moments after purchase, booking, or plan start, beside a curated supplier catalog—not as generic “clinic hub advertising.”

**Why:** The intended audience is major brands, and the owner explicitly chose both this visual system and the broader digital-plus-physical category definition while rejecting vague “premium channel” language and fake-looking proof.

**How to apply:** Use lime for meaningful actions and emphasis against navy or warm white, keep body copy high-contrast, audit mobile type scale, preserve truthful offer and performance boundaries, and label each hub as live versus signed/launching.
