---
name: Public performance claims
description: Rules for separating internal ad-planning assumptions from claims that can appear on Birch Reserve public pages.
---

The approved publication scope is verified high-level aggregate activity plus one explicitly approved rollout projection: “an initial display base expected to approach 100M annual impressions by late fall 2026.” Never shorten that to an unqualified 100M-impression claim. Internal models may contain target CTR, conversion, audience value, reach, customer names, launch queues, and other future inventory assumptions. Do not publish these as observed results or guaranteed performance unless they are substantiated by first-party data and separately approved for public use. Do not publish first-party lifetime-value dollars yet.

**Why:** Aggregate-only public use is the approved publication boundary. Early planning material mixes forecasts, private commercial context, and benchmark claims; publishing it as proof would mislead buyers and could expose partner information.

**How to apply:** Keep the approved 100M wording explicitly forward-looking, expected, and tied to late fall 2026; do not describe it as current delivery. Other public copy may use directional ranges only as clearly labeled industry-informed planning scenarios, never as observed Birch Reserve results, forecasts, or guarantees. Customer profiles may be grounded in live placement moments, but not invented census, demographic, income, age, or survey-panel claims. Keep confidential sources, unapproved exact metrics, reach claims, partner names, deal stages, and queues off public pages.
