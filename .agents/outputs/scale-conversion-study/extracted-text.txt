Product Cross-Sells vs Clinical Service Referrals
Internal Research | August 2026
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This internal study quantifies expected post-checkout conversion performance for two offer types inside the ScaleHealth
private network: product-to-product cross-sells versus clinical service referrals (primarily Physiotherapy activation into
the Align network).
Core Finding: Product cross-sells deliver higher immediate conversion (8–15% typical one-click range; H&W industry
upsell average 22.3%). Clinical service referrals convert lower on first click (3–8% baseline lead-to-book) but generate
materially higher lifetime value, exclusive patient flow into the 70+ location Align network, and brand elevation for partner
properties.
Recommendation: Run a hybrid model. Sequence a high-relevance product offer first for velocity and partner economics,
then present the clinical service as “protocol activation” or “expert next step.” Kill friction on the service side (one-tap
consult / pre-qualified booking) to close the conversion gap. Measure full contribution margin plus downstream Align
billings—not click rate alone.
Align Network Context (Current)
• 70+ active locations | Monthly gross billings tracking >$220k (March 2026) → modeled >$3.0MM calendar 2026
• ARPA stable $3.4k–$3.9k band | Median billed climbing | Core ($3k+/mo) churn <4% annualized
• 100% of locations self-fund physio costs → locked positive gross margin (30%+) on every dollar billed
• ScaleHealth demand treated as pure upside in internal models; already visible in pipeline quality

2. KEY DATA ANCHORS
Product / Post-Purchase Side
• Focus Digital 2025 (1,847 businesses): Health & Wellness upsell average 22.3%, top quartile 35.6%. Best performers:
supplement bundles, meal plans, coaching calls. Overall post-purchase upsells 14.6%.
• Shopify / Forrester 2025–26: Post-purchase one-click acceptance 6–12%; thank-you page 3–6%. Health & Supplements
cross-sell ~4.8%, upsell 5.5% in stricter definitions.
• Aftersell H&W case data: Cart upsells ~8.5%; post-purchase AOV lifts of +$3.49; pure profit per transaction $0.29–$0.80.
Service / Clinical Activation Side
• Unbounce / industry landing page data: Health & wellness median 5.1%; wellness-specific pages 8.2%.
• Physiotherapy website conversion benchmarks: average ~4.6%; optimized landing pages ~8%; Google Ads Health & Fitness
conversion ~7%.
• Telehealth / app trial-to-paid: baseline 2–5%; optimized sequences reach 8–15%. Trusted referral multiplies booking
likelihood significantly (friend referral ~4×).

3. PREDICTIVE MODEL FOR THE PRIVATE NETWORK
Inside a branded, high-trust post-checkout surface the conversion dynamics shift favorably versus open-web cold traffic.
The private network already owns the customer relationship and payment method; the only remaining variables are offer
relevance and remaining friction.
Brand Checkout Complete
→ Product Offer Path Expected 8–15%+ acceptance → Immediate cash + partner revenue share
→ Service Activation Path Expected 5–10% optimized → Align network utilization + brand elevation + multi-session LTV
Why Hybrid Wins
Pure product focus leaves the clinical asset under-monetized and fails to strengthen the Align network moat. Pure service
focus leaves easy, high-conversion post-checkout dollars on the table and weakens short-cycle partner economics.
Sequencing product first (velocity) then service as the natural clinical continuation (depth) captures both.
Clinical Adjacency Advantage
Fertility and hormonal health properties present unusually strong clinical adjacency to Physiotherapy (pelvic floor,
pregnancy-related musculoskeletal issues, hormone-driven pain and inflammation). When the service offer is framed as
the logical next step in the patient’s protocol rather than a generic referral, conversion rates compress toward the upper
end of the service range and patient quality rises.
4. STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS FOR SCALEHEALTH + ALIGN
• Partner Retention: Brands see immediate incremental revenue from product offers and elevated brand perception from
clinician activation. Both improve retention of the private network inventory.
• Align Same-Store Lift: Exclusive, high-intent patients arriving via ScaleHealth improve utilization, median billed, and
same-store growth without additional CAC.
• Network Moat: Every successful service activation strengthens the exclusive lead-flow story that differentiates ScaleHealth
from pure retail media networks.
• Operating Leverage: The model requires no material fixed-cost step-up; the existing clinical infrastructure absorbs the
incremental volume.

Three High-Leverage Levers
1. Relevance Engine (Clinical Adjacency Scoring)
Score offers by purchase + condition data. Fertility / hormonal / recovery protocols should surface Physio offers at higher
priority than generic product cross-sells. Relevance is the single largest controllable variable for service conversion.
2. Friction Kill on the Service Side
One-tap virtual consult or pre-qualified booking. Pre-fill benefit/insurance signals where possible. Brand the clinician as the
partner’s recovery specialist rather than a cold third-party referral. This is the highest-ROI conversion lever for closing the
product–service gap.
3. Full-Value Measurement
Track contribution margin per accepted offer and downstream Align billings / sessions generated / 30-60-90 day LTV of the
activated cohort. Click-to-accept alone understates the true value of the service path.
Recommended Test Design
Instrument both offer types in parallel on 2–3 live brand partners with the highest clinical adjacency (fertility, hormonal
health, recovery). Sequence product offer first, then service framed as protocol activation. Force a true A/B on service
friction (full booking flow vs one-tap consult request). Review full-value metrics at 30 days.
